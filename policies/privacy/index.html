Privacy Policy.

This page explains what information may be used to respond to enquiries, arrange consultations, and provide a paid remote service.

Last updated: 2 August 2026

1. Information collected

Depending on how you contact or book, this may include your name, contact details, company information, booking preferences, consultation notes that you choose to share, and payment or refund status.

This website does not ask for or store full card numbers, CVV codes, or banking passwords.

2. How information is used

  • To reply to enquiries and provide requested information.
  • To schedule, deliver, reschedule, or refund a consultation.
  • To maintain transaction and service records.
  • To improve the clarity and reliability of the website and service.

3. Payment and service providers

If a payment provider is used, payment details are entered on that provider's secure page and handled under its own privacy policy. Only the information needed to confirm the USD 350 transaction and deliver the service is used by Sampath Kumar.

This is a remote consulting service. No physical shipping applies because no physical product is sent.

4. Sharing and retention

Information may be shared with service providers that help with scheduling, communications, payment processing, or video calls. It is not sold as a mailing list. Information is retained only for as long as reasonably needed for the service, accounting, dispute handling, or legal obligations.

5. Your choices

You may ask what personal information is being used, request correction of inaccurate information, or ask a service-related question. Some records may need to be retained for accounting, security, or legal reasons.

6. Contact

For a privacy question, contact Sampath Kumar through LinkedIn or the phone number shown on this website.
