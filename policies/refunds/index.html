Refunds & Cancellations.

This policy explains what happens when a paid USD 350 consulting session needs to be cancelled, rescheduled, or refunded.

Last updated: 2 August 2026

1. Cancellation before the session

A client may request a full refund when they cancel at least 24 hours before the scheduled start time. The request should be made through the contact method used for the booking.

2. Rescheduling

A client may request one reschedule at least 24 hours before the session. The new time is subject to availability and should normally be arranged within 30 days of the original booking.

3. Late cancellation or no-show

Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the session, or a missed session without notice, may not qualify for a refund. Sampath Kumar may offer a reschedule at his discretion.

4. If the consultant must cancel

If Sampath Kumar cannot attend, the client may choose a replacement time or receive a full refund for the undelivered session.

5. Refund processing

Approved refunds are sent to the original payment method through the payment provider. The time for the credit to appear depends on the provider and the client's bank or card issuer. This is a remote consulting service, so no physical shipping applies.

6. Contact

To request a cancellation, reschedule, or refund, contact Sampath Kumar through LinkedIn or the phone number shown on this website. Include the booking name, scheduled time, and payment reference if available.

Payment instructions will be provided after the payment setup is approved. This website does not currently claim that a live payment link is active.
