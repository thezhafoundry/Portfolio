## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment**: This homepage is caught mid-transition between two different products. The hero, the greeting cycle, the louver-shutter physics, and the contact-card close are genuinely authored for Sampath Kumar — engineered specifically (the shutter measures the portrait's real rendered width rather than duplicating a breakpoint), not swappable onto an unrelated site. But everything added after the hero — Tools, Companies, Education, dual tickers — reads as a generic "list my stack / list my jobs" section bolted on with raw inline styles and hardcoded shadow-cards, ignoring the bespoke components (`.stack-chip`, `.offer__list`) the design system already built for exactly this content. The homepage's own DESIGN.md names this page as the flagship of the flat, no-shadow "Flat-Ruled" system — and three of its own newest sections violate that rule. The site is arguing with itself about what it is.

**Deterministic scan**: 27 findings on `index.html` alone, 90 across `src`/`public`. ~108 of the combined 117 are `design-system-font-size`/`design-system-radius` advisories, concentrated in the Tools grid's inline styles (`index.html:129-194`, the same handful of values repeated across 4 near-identical cards) — this is expected noise from DESIGN.md deliberately not tokenizing one-offs, not 108 distinct problems, and it independently confirms Assessment A's "Tools section ignores the system" finding by pointing at the exact same lines. Beyond that: 3 undocumented-color advisories on the YouTube modal (`index.html:495-496`, literal `#000`/`rgba(0,0,0,*)` instead of the documented `color-mix(...var(--color-deep-violet)...)` pattern — genuine, minor drift), and 2 confirmed `bounce-easing` warnings (`src/js/shared/magnetic.js:33`, imported by 4 of 5 page entry scripts; `src/styles/story.css:31`) with no DESIGN.md exception for either. Two `side-tab` findings (`results.css:113`, `policy.css:61`) pattern-matched correctly but are likely **false positives here** — DESIGN.md explicitly documents thin rule-lines as a deliberate structural motif in this system, not the generic decorative pattern the detector is trained to flag.

**Visual overlays**: Not available — no browser automation tool is exposed in this session, so no rendered overlay could be shown. Both assessments worked from source (HTML/CSS/JS) rather than a screenshot; treat visual-only claims (spacing rhythm, contrast) as inferred from token values, not confirmed on a rendered page.

## Overall Impression

The hero is the real thing — distinctive, engineered, on-brand. But scroll past it and the page stops being edited: a design system with real conviction gives way to bolted-on generic sections, and two "watch me" moments that are supposed to build trust for a paid consulting ask instead actively break it (one is a literal rickroll). The single biggest opportunity is triage, not redesign: fix what's broken and either integrate or cut what doesn't belong, before touching anything structural.

## What's Working

1. **The louver-hero interaction is genuinely engineered.** `louver.js` measures the portrait's actual rendered width to align the shutter's opening zone rather than duplicating a CSS value in JS, and degrades cleanly with no JS, no fine pointer, or reduced motion.
2. **Accessibility care in the greeting cycle.** A single static `aria-label` covers the whole cycling greeting so screen readers hear one sentence instead of 12 flickering ones — a detail most teams miss.
3. **Restraint on the one verified metric.** The LinkedIn follower count deliberately skips a fabricated growth-curve animation, consistent with the product's "no invented data" principle — in sharp contrast to the unsourced stats sitting right next to it (see P0 below).

## Priority Issues

**[P0] The featured "proof" video is a rickroll.**
- **What**: The "Watch Lead Gen Strategy Session ▶" button (`index.html:90`) opens YouTube ID `dQw4w9WgXcQ` — "Never Gonna Give You Up" — which is also `youtube-modal.js:13`'s hardcoded fallback ID.
- **Why it matters**: This sits in the "About Lead Gen" section, exactly where the site should be building credibility toward a $350 paid consulting ask. A prospect who clicks gets a joke instead of proof.
- **Fix**: Replace with a real intro/strategy video, or remove the button until one exists.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

**[P0] Homepage stats with no traceable source, beside a card that explicitly avoids fabrication.**
- **What**: The "Live LinkedIn impact" card (`index.html:56-75`) headlines "10,000+ connections & 2.1M+ impressions" and an "Outbound CSAT 9.2/10." Only the 8,331-follower figure is documented anywhere in PRODUCT.md's Evidence on Hand.
- **Why it matters**: PRODUCT.md states as a Product Principle that every claim must trace to a named, verifiable source. Three of four numbers on this card don't.
- **Fix**: Cite real sources for connections/impressions/CSAT, or drop them and let the one verified metric stand alone, as `/results/` already does.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] Newer homepage sections betray the page's own governing design rule.**
- **What**: `.company-card`/`.education-card` (`home.css:1234-1246`, `1298-1307`) use `box-shadow` + hardcoded `border-radius: 1.1rem`; inline `tool-category-card`/`tool-tag` markup (`index.html:129-193`) hardcodes `border-radius: 20px` and duplicates the existing, already-styled `.stack-chip` component instead of reusing it.
- **Why it matters**: DESIGN.md names this exact page as the target for the flat, no-shadow, token-driven system. The flagship page for the design language is the one breaking it.
- **Fix**: Rebuild Tools on `.stack-group`/`.stack-chip`; strip inline styles; move Companies/Education to the flat rule-and-tint language or document them as an approved exception.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify`

**[P1] Both video affordances fail silently.**
- **What**: The floating PiP widget's source `/assets/sampath-intro.mp4` (`index.html:550`) doesn't exist in `public/assets/`; `pip-video.js:41` swallows the resulting error with `.catch(() => {})` and no user feedback.
- **Why it matters**: A visitor clicks "▶ Watch intro," the card opens, and nothing happens — silently, with no error state. The second of two broken "watch me" moments on one page.
- **Fix**: Supply the real video, or hide the PiP trigger entirely until the asset exists (the code comment already implies this was the intent).
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] Simulator copy contradicts itself the instant JS loads.**
- **What**: The HTML ships "Target Leads & Prospects Volume" / "120 Leads & Prospects / mo," but `simulator.js:28`'s `update()` overwrites the badge to "120 accounts / mo" on init.
- **Why it matters**: Anyone with JS enabled — everyone — never sees the intended, deliberately-updated copy.
- **Fix**: Update the template string in `simulator.js` to match the shipped copy.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`
